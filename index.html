About me

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne, broadly interested in language, cognition, culture, and communication. I study how words and their meanings vary across languages and evolve over time, and the broader cultural, cognitive, and communicative factors that shape this variation and evolution. I use computational methods, including statistics and AI, together with information-theoretic approaches on naturalistic, large-scale data such as dictionaries and corpora to address these questions.
